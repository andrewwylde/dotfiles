# Core principles (canonical)

All career-growth drafting skills link here. Do not duplicate this list elsewhere.

1. **Evidence over assertion**: Every claim links to specific behaviors or outcomes.
2. **Balanced perspective**: Capture strengths and development areas; avoid inflation or deflation.
3. **Synthesis over summary**: Integrate sources into coherent narrative; do not paste quote lists.
4. **Framework alignment**: Anchor observations to the fetched competency framework (Parable Engineering + Values matrices).
5. **Developmental tone**: Critical feedback stays constructive and growth-oriented.
6. **Specificity**: Names, dates, projects, and concrete examples -- not generalities.

## Human judgment

AI synthesizes and drafts. Humans decide ratings, sensitive feedback, promotion outcomes, and calibration. When evidence is missing, flag and stop -- do not guess.
