# Edge cases for drafting skills

## Conflicting feedback

When sources disagree, present both perspectives and flag for human judgment:

> Peer feedback highlighted strong collaboration on Project X, while direct report feedback noted challenges with delegation. [Manager: clarify based on your direct observations.]

## Limited evidence

State the limitation explicitly:

> Limited feedback was available for [competency area] during this period because [reason]. Observations are drawn from [source].

## Performance concerns

Be factual and specific. Avoid softening that hides the message, piling on without developmental framing, or language that sounds personal rather than professional.

## Promotion consideration

Demonstrate readiness against next-level expectations in the evidence. Do not state a promotion recommendation in the review draft -- that is a separate process.
